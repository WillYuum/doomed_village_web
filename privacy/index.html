CozyLitWindow is a website promoting our game. We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the information collected when you visit.

Website Analytics

We use Umami Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand website traffic and improve our website.

Umami provides us with the following information:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the website
  • Visit timestamps, session activity, and returning visits
  • Approximate geographical location (country and city)
  • Browser, operating system, and device type
  • Referring websites and traffic sources
  • Real-time statistics about active visitors

Umami does not use tracking cookies. It uses technical information to generate privacy-focused visitor and session statistics.

We do not configure Umami to collect names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information.

We use analytics only to understand interest in CozyLitWindow, evaluate website traffic, and improve the website experience.

We do not sell analytics information or use it for personalized advertising.

Cookies

Umami Analytics implementation does not use cookies.

Data Processing

Analytics information is processed by Umami Cloud, provided by Umami Software, Inc.

Umami may process technical information, including IP addresses, to provide analytics functionality. According to Umami's documentation, raw IP addresses are not stored in analytics records.

Analytics processing may involve infrastructure outside the European Economic Area.

For more information, visit Umami.
